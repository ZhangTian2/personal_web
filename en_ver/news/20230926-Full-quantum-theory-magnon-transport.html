Full quantum theory for magnon transport in two-sublattice magnetic insulators and magnon junctions

Our paper "Full quantum theory for magnon transport in two-sublattice magnetic insulators and magnon junctions" has been published in Physical Review B (108, 104421) on September 26, 2023. This study establishes a full quantum theory framework for magnon transport in two-sublattice magnetic insulators and magnon junctions using second quantization and the nonequilibrium Green's function method.
