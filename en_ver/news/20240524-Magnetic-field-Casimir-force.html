Magnetic-field tuning of the Casimir force

Our paper "Magnetic-field tuning of the Casimir force" has been published in Nature Physics (20, 1282–1287, 2024) on May 24, 2024. This study achieves magnetic-field tuning of the Casimir force, representing an important advance at the intersection of quantum physics and magnetism.
