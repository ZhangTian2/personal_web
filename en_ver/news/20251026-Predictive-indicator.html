Predictive indicator of critical point in equilibrium and nonequilibrium magnetic systems

Our paper "Predictive indicator of critical point in equilibrium and nonequilibrium magnetic systems" has been published in Physical Review Letters on October 26, 2025. This study proposes a predictive indicator for critical points in both equilibrium and nonequilibrium magnetic systems, providing a new theoretical tool for studying magnetic phase transition dynamics.
