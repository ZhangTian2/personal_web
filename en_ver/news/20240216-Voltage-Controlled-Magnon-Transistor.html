Voltage-Controlled Magnon Transistor via Tuning Interfacial Exchange Coupling

Our paper "Voltage-Controlled Magnon Transistor via Tuning Interfacial Exchange Coupling" has been published in Physical Review Letters (132, 076701) on February 16, 2024. This study realizes a voltage-controlled magnon transistor by tuning interfacial exchange coupling, opening a new pathway for low-power manipulation of magnonic logic devices.
